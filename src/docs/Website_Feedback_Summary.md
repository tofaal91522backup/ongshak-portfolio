Review of priority improvements for Drishti ongshak and ThreadBridge

## **Additional ongshak suggestions**

Suggestions from ChatGPT

- 01 Make the homepage message clearer at first glance. “347+ Products Built. Zero Shortcuts.” is strong, but a visitor may not immediately understand what ongshak actually does. A short, clear line about the core business could help.

- 02 Review the “347+ Products” wording. Other pages refer to 347+ projects, while the homepage says 347+ products. Using one consistent term would make the message clearer.

- 03 Give more focus to business results, not only technologies. The website shows many technologies such as Next.js, Django, AWS, Flutter, STM32, etc. For senior business clients, highlighting what ongshak has achieved for clients could be more meaningful than a long technology list.

- 04 The Work page could show fewer projects with stronger stories. There are many projects followed by another detailed case-study section. Selecting the strongest projects and giving each one a clear challenge → solution → result story could make the portfolio easier to understand.

- 05 Add clearer results to case studies. Some current figures describe the client's business rather than what ongshak achieved. Where possible, showing results such as time saved, users reached, processes automated, or business improvement would better demonstrate Ongshak’s contribution.

- 06 Strengthen the contact journey. After viewing a service or case study, visitors should have a very clear next step such as “Discuss a Project” or “Talk to Our Team.” This could be placed consistently across key pages.

- 07 The AI & Innovation page feels incomplete in some areas. Computer Vision has examples and explanations, while NLP, Predictive Analytics, Intelligent Automation, and MLOps are listed with much less supporting information. Adding examples or reducing the unsupported categories would make the page feel more complete.
